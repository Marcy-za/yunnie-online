Khadgar

Khadgar is a personal assistant I run on my own home server. It is voice-first and local-first, and it keeps its own calendar and mailbox. It tracks my job search, watches my home network, reads the news I care about and, with my approval each time, can change things on my behalf. I design it and Claude writes most of the code.

Khadgar is a private, single-user project. It is not a public product and has no other users.

About the project

  • Runs on a Proxmox home server, with its own isolated sandbox for development work.
  • Every action that changes something needs my explicit approval, and is logged.
  • Reads mail through read-only access to a dedicated mailbox. See the privacy policy.
